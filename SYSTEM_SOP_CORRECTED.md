## 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

### 2.1 Project Overview
This study aims to design and develop a community interaction platform intended to enhance:
- **Information sharing** among CTU - Danao-Barangan stakeholders
- **Communication** opportunities
- **Peer connectivity** and community engagement
- **Access** to campus information as CTU becomes a technological university

### 2.2 Current Challenges
The platform addresses the disconnect users face in current academic environments by:
- Improving access to scattered or fragmented digital information
- Enhancing peer connectivity in dispersed campus environments
- Providing a centralized platform for community interaction

## 3. RESEARCH QUESTIONS

### 3.1 User Profile
- What constitutes the user profile for the platform?
- What are user demographics and characteristics?
- What are user needs and expectations?

### 3.2 Current Challenges Faced by CTU - Danao-Barangan Stakeholders

#### In terms of:

**A. Accessing Campus Information**
- Events and opportunities through existing digital platforms
- Existing digital platforms limitations
- Information accessibility gaps

**B. Connecting with Peers**
- Who share similar academic and personal interests
- Engaging with the broader university communities
- Finding like-minded individuals in a centralized manner

### 3.3 Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

**What specific features are designed and implemented in the platform that meet the following:**

#### Functional Requirements:
1. **User Management and Identity Verification** (Groups)
   - Authentication system
   - Student ID verification
   - Role-based access (Student, Leader, Admin)

2. **Interest-Based Community Circle Management**
   - Circle creation and management
   - Interest matching
   - Community discovery

3. **Real-Time Communication and Campus Feed**
   - Messaging system
   - Activity feeds
   - Notifications
   - Post and comment functionality

#### Non-Functional Requirements:
1. **Usability**
   - Intuitive interface
   - Accessibility standards
   - User experience optimization

2. **Performance**
   - Response time
   - Scalability
   - Load handling

3. **Security and Reliability**
   - Data protection
   - Privacy controls
   - System stability
   - Backup and recovery

4. **Sustainability**
   - Maintainability
   - Long-term viability
   - Resource efficiency
