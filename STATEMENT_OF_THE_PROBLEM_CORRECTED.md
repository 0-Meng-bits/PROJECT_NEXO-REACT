# STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM - CORRECTED VERSION

---

## Title:
**Innovative Development of Community Interaction Platform Assessing Usability, Accessibility, and Engagement among CTU Danao-Barangan Stakeholders**

---

## Statement of the Problem

This study aims to design and develop a community interaction platform intended to enhance information sharing, communication opportunities, and peer connectivity among CTU - Danao-Barangan stakeholders.

The disconnect manifests itself in various ways: students struggle to access campus information, events, and opportunities through existing digital platforms. Moreover, connecting with peers who share similar academic and personal interests and engaging with the broader university communities in a centralized manner remains challenging.

---

## Research Questions

Specifically, this study aims to address the following research questions:

### 1. User Profile
What constitutes the user profile for the platform?

### 2. Current Challenges
What are the current challenges faced by CTU - Danao-Barangan stakeholders in terms of:

**2.1** Accessing campus information, events, and opportunities through existing digital platforms?

**2.2** Connecting with peers who share similar academic and personal interests and engaging with the broader university communities in a centralized manner?

### 3. Platform Features and Requirements
What specific features—both Functional and Non-Functional—are designed and implemented in the platform that meet the following requirements:

**3.1** User management and identity verification (groups)?

**3.2** Interest-based community circle management?

**3.3** Real-time communication and campus feed functionality?
